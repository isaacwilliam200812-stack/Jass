About Us

Faith, discipline, and excellence in learning.

Juba Seventh-day Adventist Secondary School (JASS) is a Christian, co-educational institution located at the Seventh-day Roundabout in Munuki, Juba, South Sudan. Established in 2017 by the Seventh-day Adventist Church, the school has grown from a small initiative into a prominent and disciplined learning center known for its high academic standards and values-driven character formation.

Our mission is to provide holistic education that nurtures students in academic excellence, spiritual development, social responsibility, and personal growth. At JASS, we believe every learner deserves a supportive environment where faith, wisdom, and discipline equip them to become responsible leaders and productive citizens.

Our Values

The four pillars of our school culture.

Academic Excellence

We focus on quality learning, preparation for national examinations, and continuous student support.

Spiritual Growth

Daily chapel, prayer, Sabbath programs, and counseling help students build a strong faith foundation.

Social Growth

We develop communication, integrity, empathy, and a deep sense of responsibility to others.

Physical Development

We encourage discipline, resilience, active participation, and healthy habits that strengthen character.

Our Vision

Creating capable, disciplined, and compassionate graduates.

Our vision is to develop knowledgeable, God-fearing, and socially responsible young people who can contribute meaningfully to their homes, churches, nation, and the world.

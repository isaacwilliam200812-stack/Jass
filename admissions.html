How to Apply

Start your journey with JASS.

Admissions at JASS are handled in a simple and welcoming process. We encourage parents and guardians to secure places early, especially for high-demand classes such as Senior Four.

  • Visit the school office at the Seventh-day Roundabout, Munuki.
  • Request an admission form or complete a registration inquiry.
  • Bring supporting academic and identification records.
  • Speak with the school administration for placement and next steps.

Required Documents

What to bring when you register.

Previous Academic Records

Previous report cards or academic transcripts from the current or last school attended.

Examination Results

South Sudan Primary Leaving Examination certificate or equivalent academic evidence.

Identification

Guardian and student identification documents or copies as required by administration.

Admissions advice

We encourage early application because spaces may be limited, especially in upper classes. Families are welcome to call or visit the school for more information about available places, curriculum tracks, and school procedures.

Whether your child is interested in the Science or Arts pathway, JASS is committed to providing a supportive environment where students grow in character, confidence, and academic readiness.

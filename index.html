Welcome

Building character, confidence, and academic excellence.

JASS is a Christian, co-educational institution located at the Seventh-day Roundabout in Munuki, Juba. Since its founding, the school has grown steadily into a respected center of learning that combines academic rigor with spiritual growth and personal discipline.

Our Pillars

Everything we do is centered on excellence.

A

Academic Excellence

Structured revision programs, national examination preparation, and student tracking help our learners reach their highest potential.

S

Spiritual Growth

Daily chapel devotions, pastoral counseling, Week of Prayer, and Sabbath programs nurture faith, values, and leadership.

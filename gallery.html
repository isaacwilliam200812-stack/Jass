Welcome

Building character, confidence, and academic excellence.

JASS is a Christian, co-educational institution located at the Seventh-day Roundabout in Munuki, Juba. Since its founding, the school has grown steadily into a respected center of learning that combines academic rigor with spiritual growth and personal discipline.

Principal’s Message

We nurture every child for wisdom, service, and success.

At JASS, we believe education must go beyond classroom learning. We aim to shape students who are academically prepared, spiritually grounded, and socially responsible. Our focus is to build confident young people who can lead, serve, and contribute meaningfully to society.

Our school is committed to discipline, excellence, and the development of the whole child. We are proud to walk with families in the journey of raising future leaders and faithful citizens.
